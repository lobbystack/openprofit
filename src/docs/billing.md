# Plans

Self-hosted is free at any revenue. The hosted version is free under $2,500 MRR summed across the workspace.

| Plan | Price | Sync | For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Every 6 hours | Under $2,500 MRR |
| Indie | $19 / month | Hourly | Up to $25,000 MRR |
| Pro | $49 / month | Every 15 minutes | Above $25,000 MRR |

Every connector is in every plan. Upgrade from Settings; billing goes through Polar, which handles tax. When MRR passes the plan's cap the app shows a notice until you pick the next plan. Nothing stops syncing.
