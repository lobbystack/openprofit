# Plans and pricing

This page lists what each plan costs and includes, explains how OpenProfit measures your revenue against plan limits, and describes what happens when you pass a limit. Self-hosting is free at any revenue and has no limits.

## Plans on the hosted version

Every plan includes every connector and unlimited products. Plans differ in how often your connections sync and how much history the first sync pulls:

| Plan | Price | For workspaces with | Sync frequency | History on first sync |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Under $2,500 MRR | Every 6 hours | 12 months |
| Indie | $19 per month | Up to $25,000 MRR | Every hour | 24 months |
| Pro | $49 per month | Over $25,000 MRR | Every 15 minutes | 24 months |

Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly. The weekly email, alerts and public pages are on every plan, including Free.

## How revenue is measured

The limits use monthly recurring revenue (MRR): the monthly value of your active subscriptions, summed across every product in the workspace. OpenProfit takes MRR from your revenue connectors:

- **Stripe**: active subscriptions, with yearly and weekly prices converted to a monthly amount. Usage-based prices aren’t counted
- **Polar**: the MRR Polar reports for your organization

One-time payments don’t count toward MRR.

## When you pass a plan’s limit

Nothing stops working. Syncing continues at your plan’s frequency, and every page of the app shows a notice that links to **Settings** until you move to the next plan. OpenProfit never upgrades you automatically.

## Upgrade, downgrade or cancel

Open **Settings** and click the plan you want. Payment goes through Polar, which acts as the merchant of record and charges any sales tax or VAT that applies. To change your card, see invoices or cancel, click **Manage billing** in the same section.

When you cancel, your workspace keeps the paid plan until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free. Your data stays.
