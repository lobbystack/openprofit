# Provider logos

`ProviderLogo` (`src/components/provider-logo.tsx`) renders these files. `<id>-dark.svg` replaces `<id>.svg` in dark mode.

The logos are trademarks of their owners. OpenProfit shows them only to name the services it connects to.

SVGL ([svgl.app](https://svgl.app), MIT license, copyright Pablo Hdez) supplied most files. We downloaded them from `https://svgl.app/library/<file>` and serve our own copies. Where SVGL links a brand guidelines page, the table lists it.

We changed only the `viewBox` of `stripe`, `revenuecat`, `openai`, `resend` and `xai` to crop empty padding, so each mark fills its box at 14 to 26 px.
